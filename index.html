How it works

Four steps from first conversation to a plan that protects everyone. 

1

Continuity check

A conversation about how your business runs today, who it depends on and what would happen if you couldn't be there tomorrow.

Map key people and shareholders

Spot the gaps in your current plan

2

Structure the agreement

Agree how shares transfer, and at what value, if a trigger event happens: death, disability, exit, divorce or bankruptcy.

Set the trigger events

Agree a fair valuation method

3

Fund the plan

Choose how the payout is funded, through cross-purchase cover, company-owned policies or instalment payments.

Cross-purchase or entity purchase

Match the cover to the share value

4

Review and keep current

Revisit the plan as the business grows, partners change and valuations move, so it still works when it's needed.
